These localities impose their own income, wage, or earnings taxes independent of the state, filed on their own schedule. They are pinned on the map above - click a pin or the links below to jump to the calendar filtered to that jurisdiction. Ohio's pin covers RITA member municipalities statewide; Michigan's pin covers Michigan cities administered by the state, including Detroit.
